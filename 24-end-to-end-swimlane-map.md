# 24 — End-to-End Swimlane Map and Hours Saved

One property, intake to close. Minutes are modeled from process interviews + document-automation case studies. Replace with time-and-motion on the first live engagement.

| Process | Step | Now | Target | Hours saved / property |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intake | Tax lookup | 30–90 min | 5 min (ATTOM/Regrid) | 0.4–1.4 |
| Intake | Listing agreement | ~60 min | 15 min (PandaDoc) | 0.75 |
| Intake | Stalled signature follow-up | forgotten / ad hoc | automated reminders | leak stopped |
| Marketing | Creative build | 2–4 hr | <1 hr templates | 1–3 |
| Marketing | Channel mix | gut | rule + human approve | quality, not hours |
| Marketing | Underperformance detect | auction day | 48 hours | leak stopped |
| Buyer | Registration | spreadsheet | CRM in seconds | 0.25 |
| Buyer | Follow-up | rainmaker memory | 3-touch drip + human warm only | 0.5–1 |
| Auction | Bidding | platform | platform | 0 |
| Post-sale | Contract re-key | 45–120 min | 0 (export → PandaDoc) | 0.75–2 |
| Post-sale | Data sheet re-entry | 20–40 min | 0 | 0.3–0.7 |
| Post-sale | Bank reconcile | manual | Plaid/feed | 0.25 |

**Modeled total:** 4–6 hours of manual work removed per property.
Across a 43-campaign cycle that is ~170–260 hours — nearly a full-time year recovered if volume holds.
Do not treat this as a guaranteed savings number. It is the sizing model for Stage 02 business case.
