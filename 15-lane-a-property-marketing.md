- 1.5 Evidence review against benchmarks.

- D1.4 Statement of Work — master, all five stages. Gate G1.

- 2.4 Measurement lock — primary metric views-to-downloads then cost-per-registered-bidder and cost-per-hammer. Owner named. Data source locked. Gate G2.
