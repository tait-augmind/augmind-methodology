# 05 — Stage 02 — Design and Plan

**Status:** Sourced draft — advisory only, client-agnostic.

## Decision
Is the plan actionable, measurable, and within scope?

## Work
- 2.1 Business case and future-state operating model — quantify the gap between current and future state and define how the business will operate after change.
- 2.2 Implementation roadmap and action plan — sequence the workstreams with owners, dependencies, and timing.
- 2.3 Measurement lock — success metric in the client's words, data source, and named owner. No metric, no implementation.
- 2.4 Change-control process defined — any scope move requires a written Change Control Note: what changed, why, the evidence, and who accepted it. Nothing moves without it.

## Deliverable
D2.1: plan with locked metric, source, and owner, plus the change-control process.

## Gate
G2: client signs the plan. Silence is not acceptance.
