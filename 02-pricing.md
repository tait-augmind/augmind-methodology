## Summary (agnostic)
- Price named people at published rates by level. A day is eight hours; half-day is four.
- Partner $3,000/day; Director/engagement lead $2,400/day; Subject-matter expert $2,400–$3,000/day; Manager $2,000/day; Senior consultant/specialist $1,600/day; Consultant/specialist $1,200/day; Analyst $900/day.
- Four ways to buy: Diagnostic, Fixed-fee project, Range with cap, Capacity.
- Diagnostic: Focused $11,100 (one week); Full $17,500 (two weeks). Credited in full against follow-on work within 90 days.
- Capacity example: director one day/week ≈ $10,400/month; senior specialist 20 hours/month = $4,000/month.
- Tools inside the rate. Client-owned software, platforms, ad spend, and data passed through at cost with zero markup.
- No rate discounts. If the number must come down, scope shrinks.
- Partners adopt the rate card before any Statement of Work ships. Client never sees "proposed."
- Recurring tier supports the Validate and Run model.
