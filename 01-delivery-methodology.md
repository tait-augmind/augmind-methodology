# 01 — Delivery Methodology

**Status:** Draft — advisory only, client-agnostic, numbered for traceability.

## 00 — Purpose
This is the reusable method for advisory engagements. It is not client-specific. Tranzon Key is the first application, not the definition.

## 01 — Three Frameworks
1. Structured listening — capture the client's situation in their own words before any solution is offered.
2. Structured problem decomposition — break the gap between current state and future state into manageable workstreams.
3. Advisory gates — every stage has a decision, a deliverable, and a gate where the client can accept or stop.

## 02 — Delivery Stages

### Stage 01 — Discover and Recommend
Decision: Is there a defined problem worth solving, and is the client ready to act?

Work:
- 1.1 Structured listening
- 1.2 Current-state assessment — data-driven baseline of operations, finances, processes, environment
- 1.3 Future-state definition
- 1.4 Structured problem decomposition
- 1.5 Evidence review against benchmarks
- 1.6 Recommendation

Deliverable D1.4: single Statement of Work covering all five stages.
Gate G1: client accepts the recommendation and signs the Statement of Work, or names specific gaps. Silence is not acceptance.

### Stage 02 — Design and Plan
Decision: Is the plan actionable, measurable, and within scope?

Work:
- 2.1 Business case and future-state operating model
- 2.2 Implementation roadmap and action plan
- 2.3 Measurement lock — success metric in the client's words, data source, named owner
- 2.4 Change-control process defined

Deliverable D2.1: plan with locked metric, source, and owner.
Gate G2: client signs the plan.

### Stage 03 — Implement
Decision: Are the processes live and documented?

Work:
- 3.1 Detailed process designs
- 3.2 Organizational and technological requirements
- 3.3 Results Management Office to drive, coordinate, support, measure, and track

Deliverable D3.1: live processes and documented runbook.
Gate G3: first cycle shows movement on the locked metric.

### Stage 04 — Validate and Run
Decision: Can the client accept the result, and is it ready to run under AugMind?

Work:
- 4.1 Check against acceptance criteria
- 4.2 Transfer ownership and access
- 4.3 Stand up the repeatable process — automation, monitoring, operating cadence

Deliverable D4.1: accepted deliverables plus a running process with named owners, support boundaries, and a recurring operating plan.
Gate G4: client accepts the run state or names gaps. Running starts only after acceptance.

### Stage 05 — Review results and improve
Decision: Is the engagement producing measurable improvement?

Work:
- 5.1 Trend analysis across cycles
- 5.2 Refine targeting and update benchmark comparison
- 5.3 Quality review — risk-based, one named reviewer outside the delivery team on higher-value work

Deliverable D5.1: improvement report and next-cycle plan.
Gate G5: named reviewer signs off before anything returns to the client.

## 03 — Quality Gate (Advisory-Grade)
Not audit. Risk-based review by one named person outside the delivery team on higher-value or higher-risk work. KPMG Advisory Quality Performance Reviews are performed by the Functional Quality and Risk Management Partner, rated green, yellow, or red on engagement set-up and execution. Source: KPMG Integrated Report 2024/2025.

## 04 — Pricing
Partners adopt the rate card before any Statement of Work ships. Client never sees "proposed." Four ways to buy: diagnostic, fixed-fee project, range with cap, capacity. Recurring tier for the run model. No rate discounts — scope shrinks instead.
