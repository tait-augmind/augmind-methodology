# 06 — Stage 03 — Implement

**Status:** Sourced draft — advisory only, client-agnostic.

## Decision

## Work
- 3.1 Detailed process designs — define the target processes in enough detail to operate and hand over.
- 3.2 Organizational and technological requirements — identify what people, roles, and systems must change.
