# 21 — D1.1 Current-State Assessment (synthetic filled)

**Stage 01 / work item 1.2.** Traces to KPMG data-driven baseline method.

## 1. Company profile
Regional real estate auction firm, Mid-Atlantic territory, part of an eight-company national franchise. Three rainmaker partners plus a small marketing team and a long-tenured solo IT consultant. Modeled cycle: 43 campaigns, $252,038 proposed marketing spend.

## 2. Financial baseline (modeled from de-identified spend shape)
- Total proposed marketing spend: $252,038.
- One originator holds ~61% of spend across ~20 campaigns.
- Two others together ~26%; remainder scattered one-offs.
- Median campaign budget ~$5,800.
- Many budgets sit on round seller-agreed caps — built backward from price, not expected yield.

## 3. Channel baseline (modeled)
- Google Ads ~19.2% of portfolio.
- Meta ~14.2%.
- Direct mail ~18.1%.
- Signage ~10.8%.
- Production and supplements ~16.1%.
- Owned email ~0.4% — the unbuilt layer.

## 4. Channel mix finding
Google already leads Meta in 42 of 43 modeled campaigns. Do not sell a “shift spend to Google” story. The live gap is measurement, not mix.

## 5. Process baseline — marketing
Seller cap → originator reverse-engineers budget → marketing places ads → dashboard shows views → packages downloaded → auction. Bottleneck is views-to-downloads. No UTM taxonomy, no bidder registration tracking, no cost-per-registered-bidder.

## 6. Process baseline — seller/referral
Relationships live in rainmaker heads. Referral arrives by phone/email. No CRM, no drip, no source ROI. Industry: a large share of auctioneer selection is recommendation-driven; this firm has no system to capture or nurture it.

## 7. Process baseline — paperwork
Tax records pulled manually. Listing agreement drafted by hand. Fieldwork form repeated per property. Purchase contract fields filled manually. Data sheets compiled again for marketing. No version control.

## 8. Gaps ranked
1. Measurement architecture.
2. Owned email.
3. Process automation that survives the rainmakers.

## 9. Evidence rule
Every finding traces to spend file, call notes, published benchmarks, or process SOP sources. Unsourced findings do not ship.
