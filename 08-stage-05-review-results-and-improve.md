# 08 — Stage 05 — Review Results and Improve

**Status:** Sourced draft — advisory only, client-agnostic.

## Decision
Is the engagement producing measurable improvement?

## Work
- 5.1 Trend analysis across cycles — compare accumulated results against the locked baseline and benchmark set.
- 5.2 Refine targeting and update benchmark comparison — adjust channel mix, creative, or process based on evidence.
- 5.3 Quality review — risk-based, one named reviewer outside the delivery team on higher-value or higher-risk work.

## Deliverable
D5.1: improvement report and next-cycle plan.

## Gate
G5: named reviewer signs off before anything returns to the client.
