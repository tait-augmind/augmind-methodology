## Four ways to buy
1. Diagnostic — fixed fee, credited in full against follow-on work. Covers Stage 01–02.
2. Fixed-fee project — Stages 01–05 build, one-time.
3. Range with cap — Stage 03 implementation, capped.
4. Capacity / retainer — Stage 04 onward, monthly or quarterly, tied to live processes.
