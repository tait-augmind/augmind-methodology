# 17 — Lane C: Paperwork & Data Entry (synthetic)

**Lane owner:** AI/Process Lead + Digital Specialist.

## Stage 01
- Inventory forms: fieldwork, purchase contracts, data sheets, tax-record extracts.
- Opportunity sizing: hours per form, monthly volume, error cost.
- Benchmark: 40+ hours/month saved; 95–97% first-pass accuracy; 20–40 min → under 2 min.
- Future-state: AI-assisted form completion from tax records + listing agreement. Gate G1.

## Stage 02
- Tool selection, field mapping, validation rules.
- Who reviews, who signs, escalation path.
- Measurement lock: hours saved/month; first-pass accuracy %; error rate. Gate G2.

## Stage 03
- Connect tax-record and listing-agreement sources.
- 30-minute onboarding per user.
- Weekly first-pass review. Gate G3: accuracy ≥ 95% over two weeks.

## Stages 04–05
Actual hours saved vs 40+/month; runbook; retainer; trend; external sign-off.
